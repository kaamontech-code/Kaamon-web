KaamOn is a local platform that connects cafes, restaurants and event venues with verified and approved nearby servers, waiters and general helpers on an hourly or daily basis, according to your required day and time, so busy rush hours get covered at minimal cost, helping your business grow and become more profitable.

It also helps people who need extra income earn it through flexible shifts.
