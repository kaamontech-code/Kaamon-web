Two simple ways in

One platform, built for both sides of a shift.

For businesses

Short of staff? Fill a shift in minutes.

  1. 1Sign in with Google and get approved by our team.
  2. 2Post a shift: date, time, pay, location and how many workers you need.
  3. 3Workers accept. Share the OTP on arrival, then pay by cash or UPI.
For businesses

For waiters and helpers

Earn extra income on shifts that fit your day.

  1. 1Sign in with Google and get approved after a quick phone check.
  2. 2Browse open shifts and see the time, pay and map location before you accept.
  3. 3Reach on time, enter the OTP, work, and get paid by the business.
For workers

Built so shifts actually happen

Every step of a shift is recorded, so both sides know what to expect.

01Approved people only

Our team approves every worker and business after a phone check.

02Everything up front

Date, time, pay, dress code and a Google Maps location, before anyone accepts.

03OTP check-in

A 4-digit code from the business confirms the worker has arrived.

04Live work clock

Start and finish times are stamped by the server, so hours are never in doubt.

05Cash or UPI

Pay in cash or scan a UPI QR. The worker confirms once the payment is received.

06Ratings and comments

Businesses and workers rate each other after every shift and build a reputation.
