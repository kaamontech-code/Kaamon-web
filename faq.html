What is KaamOn?

KaamOn is a local platform that connects cafes, restaurants and venues with part-time waiters and general helpers for single shifts. It is currently in pilot in Koramangala, Bengaluru.

Who can use it?

Businesses that need short-term staff, and waiters or general helpers looking for part-time shifts. Everyone is approved by our team before using the app.

How are people approved?

You sign in with Google, add your name and mobile number, and we call or message you to verify. Only approved accounts can post or accept shifts.

Does it cost anything?

KaamOn is free to try during the pilot. If fees are introduced later, we will tell you before they apply.

How does payment work?

The business pays the worker directly, by cash or UPI. KaamOn does not handle the money. The business marks the shift paid and the worker confirms receipt in the app.

What is the OTP for?

When a worker accepts a shift, the business gets a 4-digit OTP. The worker enters it when they arrive. It proves they showed up and starts the work clock.

What if a worker does not show up?

Contact us and we can reset the shift so another worker can take it. Ratings and comments also help businesses choose reliable workers.

Can I hire more than one worker?

Yes. When posting a shift you can ask for up to 20 workers, and each worker accepts, checks in and is paid separately.

How do I install the app?

Approved businesses and workers get the app link from us. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu and choose Install app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and choose Add to Home Screen. In Samsung Internet, open the link in Chrome to install.

Is my information safe?

We collect only what the service needs and keep it with Google's secure services. Read the privacy policy for details.
