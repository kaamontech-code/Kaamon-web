Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

KaamOn is a pilot service in Bengaluru that connects businesses with part-time workers. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email from Google sign-in, your role, and the mobile number you provide.
  • Shift details: shifts you post or accept, times worked, map locations, dress code and notes.
  • Payment details you choose to share: your payment method and UPI ID. We do not process payments or store card or bank account numbers.
  • Ratings and comments you give or receive.

How we use it

To approve accounts, run shifts, show the right information to the right people, resolve problems, and improve KaamOn.

Who sees it

  • Businesses and workers on a shift see each other's names and ratings. A worker who accepts a shift also sees the business's contact number for that shift. A business sees the worker's chosen payment method and UPI ID.
  • Our team can see all account and shift records to run the pilot.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google services (Firebase) and may be exported by our team to Google Sheets for records and reporting.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information through the Contact page. Some shift records may be kept where needed to resolve disputes.

Changes

We may update this policy as KaamOn grows and will change the date above when we do.
