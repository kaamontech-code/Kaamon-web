How it works

From shift to payment in eight steps

Every step is recorded, so businesses and workers always know where a shift stands.

  1. Business posts a shiftBusiness

    Pick the worker type, date, start time, hours, pay, how many workers you need and a Google Maps location.

  2. Workers see it and acceptWorker

    Approved waiters or helpers see the time, pay and location, then accept. Workers choose cash or UPI at this step.

  3. OTP check-inBoth

    The business gets a 4-digit OTP once someone accepts. The worker enters it on arrival to start the shift.

  4. The work clock runsBoth

    Start time is stamped by the server and a live clock runs on both screens until the business confirms the finish.

  5. Business confirms the finishBusiness

    One tap closes the shift for each worker, with the total time worked recorded.

  6. PaymentBusiness

    The business pays in cash or by scanning the worker's UPI QR, then taps Mark paid.

  7. Worker confirms receiptWorker

    The worker confirms the payment arrived, so both sides have a clear record.

  8. Ratings and commentsBoth

    Each side rates the other with stars and an optional comment, building a reputation over time.
