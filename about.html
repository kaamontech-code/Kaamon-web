About

Reliable help, when you need it

KaamOn started with a simple problem: busy venues often run short of staff at the last minute, while capable people want flexible work.

What we are building

A simple, trusted way to match short shifts with approved workers. Businesses see who is coming and what they will pay. Workers see where, when and how much before they say yes.

How we keep it trustworthy

  • Every business and worker is approved by our team after a phone check.
  • An OTP at check-in and a server-stamped work clock keep records honest.
  • Payments are confirmed by both sides, and ratings build reputation.

Where we are

KaamOn is in pilot in Koramangala, Bengaluru, starting with waiters and general helpers. We are learning from every shift and improving the product week by week.
